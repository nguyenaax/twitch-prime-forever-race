The race

Each icon sits at its character's level. The trail behind it is the last 24 hours. Tap a name to open their climb chart.

Where levels come from

Once an hour, the guild roster is read from the Battle.net API. Each read is a snapshot, and the snapshots are the history behind the trails, the climb chart and the projections.

Icons move on logout

Blizzard's character data usually refreshes when a player logs out, so an icon can sit still through a long session and jump forward when they log off or take a break.

Pace is counted in XP

Level 59 to 60 takes about 28 times the XP of level 10 to 11 (vanilla's XP table, standing in for Forever's). The ETA assumes the last 24 hours' pace holds, sleep included.
